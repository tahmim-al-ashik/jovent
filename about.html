Our Story

Redefining the Modern Gentleman.

Jovent began with a simple frustration: finding menswear in Bangladesh that looked premium, fit properly and didn’t cost a fortune. So we built it ourselves — a house where tailoring discipline meets street-ready ease.

Every Jovent piece is designed in our Dhaka studio, cut from honestly-sourced fabrics and finished to a standard we’d wear ourselves. No shortcuts, no markup games — just clothing that earns its place in your wardrobe.
