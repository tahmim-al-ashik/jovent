Privacy Policy

Your data, treated like our fabrics — with care.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide at checkout or registration (name, phone, email, address).
  • Order history, so we can serve you and handle exchanges.
  • Browsing preferences stored in your own browser (bag, wishlist) to keep the site fast.

How we use it

To fulfil and deliver your orders, confirm them by phone, handle exchanges, and — only if you opt in — send you new-drop announcements. We never sell your data.

Payments

Card and bKash payments are processed by SSLCommerz over encrypted connections. We never see or store your full card number.

Cookies

We use a small cookie to remember your consent choice, plus browser storage for your bag and wishlist. No third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

Ask us any time to see, correct or delete your personal data: hello@joventmenswear.com.
